Work with me

Start with one module. Keep it if it earns its keep.

Two simple packages: a fixed-price first build, then a monthly fee to keep it running. No long contracts, no software to subscribe to.

Packages

Package 1

First build

Fixed price, quoted after a free call

  • One module set up in your business: email intake, a daily digest, status updates or payment proofing
  • Built on the tools you already use (Gmail, Google Sheets, AppSheet, n8n or Apps Script)
  • Runs in shadow mode first, logging what it would do and changing nothing
  • Switched on only after you've seen it handle your real work correctly
  • A rollback copy of every version, and plain-English notes on how it works

Package 2

Run & maintain

Monthly fee

  • Daily monitoring, with alerts when something fails
  • Fixes, plus small changes as your business changes
  • A short monthly report: what ran, what broke, what improved
  • One change a day at most, each checked before it goes live
  • Cancel any month; everything built is yours to keep

How it works

  1. 1

Questions owners ask

Do I have to switch software?

No. I build on what you already use where I can, such as Gmail, Google Sheets, AppSheet or your helpdesk. The goal is less retyping, not another app to learn.

What if it makes a mistake?

It's built to stop and ask a person whenever something is unclear, and every version can be rolled back. Read the safety method.

Who owns it?

You do. The automations run in your accounts, and you keep everything if you stop the monthly plan.

What does it cost to run?

Usually very little on top of the tools you already pay for. AI reading of an email costs about a cent. See the case study for real numbers.
