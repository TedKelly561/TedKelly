Assumptions

Estimates only; your real numbers are measured in shadow mode before anything goes live. Every reduction below is set lower than the demos show, on purpose.

  • M1 email intake: retyping drops to about 30 seconds of review per order, as in the M1 demo (from ~5 minutes). Only 80% of orders are counted, in case some still need a person.
  • M3 status updates: half of "where's my tech?" calls and emails go away. In the M3 demo thread, follow-ups went from 4 to 0; that's illustrative.
  • M4 payment proofing: 75% of checking time is saved and half of payment mistakes are caught. The M4 demo shows 2 hours per pay run cut to 5 minutes of exceptions, for an illustrative 5-tech shop.
  • M5 Zapier bill: 40% of the bill moves to scripts that cost nothing per run. In the case study the business dropped one Zapier plan tier, about $60 a month.
  • M2 health check: no dollar figure. It reduces the risk of an automation failing quietly; it doesn't remove a task.
  • A month is 4.33 weeks. Hours are multiplied by your labor cost per hour. Running costs (about a cent of AI per email, a small server if needed) and the build itself are not subtracted.
